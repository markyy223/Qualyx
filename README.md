## 🛡️ Troubleshooting Tips

Running into a snag? Don't worry. Here are answers to common questions.

**My computer doesn't see my headset.**
Make sure you've turned on Developer Mode in your headset settings and that you've allowed USB debugging on the connection prompt. Try a different USB port on your computer if you're still having issues.

**The installation keeps failing.**
Ensure you have a stable connection between your headset and PC. Try a different USB cable if you have one. Also, make sure your headset's battery is sufficiently charged.

**The game is stuttering or lagging.**
Close any other apps running in the background on your headset (press the Oculus button and select "Exit" from any app you're not using). Make sure your headset is charged and not overheating.

**I can't find Qualyx after installing it.**
Sometimes the app appears in a different library tab. On your headset, go to your library and check the "Unknown Sources" filter, which you can find in the dropdown menu at the top of the screen.
